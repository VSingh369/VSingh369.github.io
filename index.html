About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow under the DST WISE programme at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Allahabad. My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Generative AI, Multimodal Learning, and Human-Centered Computing. My long-term goal is to develop intelligent systems that improve healthcare, accessibility, education, and cognitive development.
