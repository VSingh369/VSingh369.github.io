## Professional Summary

Artificial Intelligence researcher with over **13 years of academic experience** in teaching, research, and mentoring. Currently serving as a **DST WISE-SCOPE Postdoctoral Fellow** at **IIIT Allahabad**, conducting research on AI-enhanced communication and cognitive development technologies for children with congenital hearing impairment.

My research focuses on **Generative AI, Computer Vision, Human-Centered AI, Assistive Technologies, Deep Learning, Image Captioning, Medical AI, Multimodal Learning, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)**. I am passionate about developing socially impactful AI systems and translating research into real-world applications.
